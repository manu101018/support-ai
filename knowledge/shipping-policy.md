# Shipping Policy

## Shipping Timeframes
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping (available
at checkout for an additional fee) takes 1-2 business days.

## Order Processing
Orders are marked "processing" for up to 24 hours before shipping, then
move to "shipped" with a tracking number emailed to the customer.

## Shipping Coverage
We currently ship only within India. International shipping is not
available at this time.
