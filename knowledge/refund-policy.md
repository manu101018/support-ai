# Refund Policy

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after the returned item
is received and inspected.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout.
Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded via bank transfer, which requires
the customer to provide bank account details.

If a payment was deducted but the order was never confirmed, the amount
is automatically reversed within 3-5 business days. If it has not
reversed after 5 business days, escalate to a human agent.
